Why trust us with your customers?

Because the product is built to be checked, not just believed.

The assistant admits what it doesn't know

Answers come only from your documents, with sources shown. When it doesn't know, it says so in wording you choose — a wrong answer to a customer costs you more than no answer.

We will not gate your reviews

Every customer who responds is offered your Google review link, whatever they scored. Filtering out unhappy customers breaks Google's rules, and the roadmap says so publicly.

Your page waits until it's ready

Your page stays out of Google until it has real content — an intro, a photo, a priced service. A half-finished page in search results does you harm, so we don't publish one.

Your customer list is yours

Contacts exports to CSV whenever you like, on every plan. A customer list you cannot take with you is not really yours.

Changes are written down

Every customer-visible change ships with a changelog entry, and the roadmap lists what we are not building, in public, so you can plan around us.

Honest when something's off

If an email can't send or a feature isn't configured yet, the dashboard says exactly that — not a spinner, not a silent failure.

Questions

Getting started

Do I need a website?

No. Every workspace gets a real page at makerbay.app with your services, hours and a booking button, plus a hosted chat page you can share as a link or QR code. If you do have a site, adding the chat bubble is one line of HTML.

I'm not technical. How hard is this?

If you can attach a file to an email, you can set this up. Nothing needs code except the optional chat bubble, which is one line your web person pastes once.

How long until it's working?

About ten minutes: create the workspace, add your FAQ or price list, try it in the playground, share the link.

The assistant

What happens when it doesn't know something?

It says so, in wording you choose, rather than guessing. Those questions are collected for you so you can answer them once and have it handled from then on.

Will it invent prices or policies?

No. Every reply is grounded in the documents you uploaded, with sources shown.

Pricing and plans

What's actually free?

Your page, Contacts, Requests, Quotes with invoices, Get found and the help centre — free, with no trial clock. You pay for the assistant, Bookings and Reviews, and only once you switch them on.

What happens when I hit a limit?

You are told in the dashboard, plainly, before anything stops. The caps exist to bound our costs, not to push you into upgrading.

Your data

Is my information kept separate from other businesses?

Yes. Every document, contact and conversation is tied to your workspace, and answers are only ever drawn from your own documents.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Contacts exports to CSV whenever you like, on every plan. A customer list you cannot take with you is not really yours.

Can my developer use it directly?

Yes. Everything the dashboard does is available over a REST API with your own keys.
