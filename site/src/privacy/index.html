Privacy Policy

Version 1.0. In effect from 29 August 2026.

The short version

  • We sell nothing to anybody. No advertising, no data brokers, no tracking across the web.
  • The website sets no cookies at all, and there is no Google Analytics or similar on it.
  • Your data is stored in the United States, on Amazon Web Services. If that matters to you, read section 5.
  • Two companies can touch it: Amazon Web Services, and Stripe for payments. That is the whole list.
  • The AI assistant runs inside our own AWS account. Nothing you or your customers type is used to train anybody’s model.
  • Ask us and we will send you everything we hold, or delete it.

1. Two different jobs we do

This policy covers two groups of people, and our responsibilities are genuinely different for each. It is worth two minutes to understand which one you are.

If you run a business on MakerBay, we decide how your account information is handled, so we are what data protection law calls the controller. This policy is our agreement with you about it.

If you are a customer of a business that uses MakerBay — you booked a plumber, you were sent a quote — then that business decided to store your details, not us. They are the controller. We are the processor: we hold and handle the information on their instructions and do nothing else with it.

That matters practically. If you want a business to delete what it holds about you, ask that business, because it is theirs to delete. If you cannot reach them, contact us and we will help you get to them.

2. What we collect from a business owner

  • Account. Your name, email address, phone number, business name, trading address and the modules you have turned on.
  • Billing. Your subscription status and history. Payments are handled by Stripe, so we never see or store your card number.
  • What you put in. Your contacts, bookings, quotes, invoices, price list, photos and page settings.
  • Support. Tickets you open and our replies.
  • Technical. Sign-in times, IP addresses on security-relevant actions, and error logs. We keep these to stop fraud and to fix things.

3. What we hold about your customers

Only what the business put in, or what the customer gave the business: typically a name, an email address or phone number, an address if there is a job at one, what they asked for, and the history of the messages and documents between them.

We also record whether an email reached them. If a message bounces or somebody marks it as spam, we record that against the address so we stop sending to it. This exists to protect people from being emailed repeatedly by a business that cannot reach them.

That record is kept per business, not across all of them. Two tradespeople may share a customer, and one business’s bounce must not silence that address for another business with its own relationship.

4. Why we are allowed to hold it

  • To provide the service you asked for — performance of our contract with you.
  • To keep it secure and working, and to prevent abuse — our legitimate interests.
  • To meet tax and accounting obligations — legal obligation.
  • Where you have said yes to something specific — consent, which you can take back at any time.

We do not use anyone’s data for advertising, and we do not build profiles of your customers.

5. Where your data lives, and why we are telling you plainly

MakerBay runs on Amazon Web Services in the US East (Northern Virginia) region. So if your business is in Australia, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Canada or New Zealand, your data and your customers’ data are stored in and accessed from the United States.

We are stating that prominently rather than burying it, because it is the single fact in this policy most likely to matter to you. Australian Privacy Principle 8 requires us to disclose it. UK and EU law requires an approved transfer mechanism, and we rely on the standard contractual clauses in our agreement with Amazon Web Services.

If your business needs its data kept inside a particular country, tell us before you sign up, because right now we cannot offer that.

6. Who else can touch it

The complete list. There is no one else.

  • Amazon Web Services — hosting, databases, file storage, sending email and SMS, and running the AI assistant. United States.
  • Stripe — subscription payments, and payments from your customers to you. Stripe handles card details directly, and we never receive them. United States and Ireland.

We will update this list before adding anyone, not after. We may also disclose information if the law genuinely requires it, and unless we are forbidden from doing so, we will tell you first.

If our business is ever sold, data may transfer with it. You would be told, and this policy would continue to apply until you were given notice of a new one.

7. The AI assistant

The assistant that answers enquiries on your booking page uses a Claude model from Anthropic, run through Amazon Bedrock inside our own AWS account.

  • Conversations are not used to train Anthropic’s models, or anyone else’s.
  • The content stays within our AWS account in the region named above.
  • The assistant only sees the information for the one business whose page it is answering for.
  • Conversations are stored so the business can read them back, and they are deleted when the business deletes them or closes its account.

No decision with a legal or similarly significant effect on anybody is made automatically. The assistant answers questions and takes booking details; a person decides about the work.

8. Email and text messages

Messages are sent through Amazon SES and Amazon Pinpoint. We record whether each one was delivered, bounced, or was reported as spam, so a business can see that a customer never got their invoice, and so we stop sending to addresses that do not work.

Optional messages, meaning review requests and the daily digest, carry an unsubscribe link and the one-click unsubscribe that Gmail and Apple Mail show. Acting on it is immediate and needs no confirmation step.

Messages you are waiting for, such as a quote, an invoice, a booking confirmation or a sign-in code, do not carry an unsubscribe link, because opting out of the document you are waiting for is not a useful choice. To stop those, ask the business directly.

9. Cookies

This website sets no cookies. There is no Google Analytics, no advertising pixel, and no third-party tracker of any kind on it. That is why you have not been shown a cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

When you sign in to the app, your browser stores a sign-in token so you are not asked for your password on every page. It stays on your device, is not a tracking cookie, and signing out removes it.

10. How long we keep things

  • While your account is open, we keep your data so the product works. Quotes and invoices are records you may need for years, so we do not quietly age them out.
  • When you close your account, we delete everything within 90 days, or sooner if you ask.
  • Billing records are kept for seven years, because Australian tax law requires it.
  • Security and audit logs are kept for 12 months.
  • Suppression records, meaning “do not email this address”, are kept until the account is deleted. Deleting the record would mean starting to email someone again who asked us to stop.

11. What you can ask us for

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • send you a copy of everything we hold about you;
  • correct anything wrong;
  • delete it;
  • send it to you in a portable format, or send it on to someone else;
  • stop or limit a particular use;
  • withdraw consent you gave earlier.

Email support@makerbay.app. We answer within 30 days and we do not charge for it. We may need to check who you are first, which protects you.

If you are a customer of a business that uses MakerBay, send your request to that business. If they do not respond, contact us and we will help.

12. Keeping it safe

  • Everything is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Each business’s data is separated, and every request is checked against the workspace it belongs to.
  • Databases have point-in-time recovery, so data can be restored after a mistake.
  • Staff access is limited to what the job needs and is recorded in an audit log.
  • Passwords are handled by Amazon Cognito. We never see them.

No system is perfect. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the relevant regulator within the time the law requires, and we will tell you what actually happened rather than a form of words.

13. Children

MakerBay is for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data about children. If a business has recorded a child’s details as part of a job, that is the business’s responsibility under its own obligations.

14. Changes

We will post a new version here with a new date. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we will email you at least 30 days beforehand.

15. Contact, and complaining about us

Email support@makerbay.app, or write to Appa Technologies Pty Ltd, 4 Greenwood Place, Freshwater NSW 2096, Australia.

We would rather you told us first and gave us the chance to fix it.
