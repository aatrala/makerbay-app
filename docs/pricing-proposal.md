# Pricing proposal - tiers + pay-as-you-go

Status: **APPROVED 2026-08-24 (Variant A) and implemented.** Founder decisions: USD worldwide (no regional pricing); free caps stay at 20/mo with honest framing; annual $290 with month-to-month headline; Genie taster tiers Free 25 / Trade 250 / Genie 2,500 msgs per month when Genie ships. ## Why module-based pricing is wrong for this audience

A tradie doesn't buy modules; they buy "the phone stops interrupting me" and
"I get paid faster." Module pricing (a) recreates the add-on stacking the
market research shows is the number-one competitor resentment (Vagaro, Tidio,
Calendly); (b) forces each module to survive a worth-it test alone instead of
bundling into an unbeatable Jobber comparison; (c) contradicts the platform
pitch of native inter-module flow; (d) risks unpredictable bills - metering
is only legitimate where OUR marginal cost is real (assistant messages, voice
minutes), never on bookings/quotes/invoices/review invites (a tax on their
own success, the hated hipages model).

## Variant A - "Match Jobber" (RECOMMENDED): Free / Trade $29 / Genie $99

| | Free $0 | Trade $29/mo | Genie $99/mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pitch | "Run your business online. Actually free." | "Everything switched on, for what Jobber charges to start." | "An office manager for less than one billable hour a week." |
| Included | Your page, Contacts, Requests, Quotes + invoices (200/mo), Get found, Help centre, Stripe payments (no platform fee), 200 assistant msgs/mo, 20 bookings/mo, 20 review invites/mo | Everything in Free + UNLIMITED bookings, review invites, quotes/invoices; 2,000 assistant msgs; custom domain; missed-call rescue with 100 voice min/mo (when live) | Everything in Trade + Genie conversational admin; 6,000 msgs; 300 voice min/mo; priority support |
| Metered | none (polite hard stop) | $0.02/msg; $0.15/voice min | $0.02/msg; $0.12/voice min |

Overage is opt-in per workspace (default = polite stop at the cap), keeping
"no surprise bills" literal.

## Variant B - Good-Better-Best: Free / Solo $19 / Trade $49 / Genie $99

Solo $19: unlimited bookings/reviews/quotes, 500 msgs. Trade $49: + 2,500
msgs, custom domain, rescue with 150 min. Captures more willingness-to-pay
spread and gives a cheap uncapping step - but four tiers is one decision too
many for this buyer, and $49 sits above Jobber ($29) and ServiceM8 ($32)
without a proven reason.

## Variant C - Flat + Wallet: Free / MakerBay $39

One flat paid tier (everything incl. Genie-lite, 1,000 msgs) + a prepaid
usage wallet at cost-plus rates that never auto-refills. Maximum honesty,
minimum SKUs - but strands the $99 Genie ambition and prepaid wallets are
friction for "set and forget" buyers.

## Recommendation: Variant A

$29 is a deliberate head-to-head with Jobber's entry price while including
AI, reviews and a customer page Jobber doesn't have; two paid tiers is one
decision; Genie slots in as the natural top tier; you can insert a tier above
later but never gracefully below.

## Promises kept / migration

- Free stays free: the Free tier is today's free surface verbatim; existing
  free workspaces lose nothing, ever.
- No review gating - unchanged, stated on the pricing page.
- "Caps bound costs, not upgrades" stays true for messages/minutes; be honest
  that the free 20/mo bookings and review-invite caps are VALUE lines, and
  say so plainly on the page (or raise them) - never hide behind the cost
  framing.
- Existing Assistant Pro ($29) subscribers auto-map to Trade at $29 -
  strictly more for the same money ("your plan got bigger, your price
  didn't"). Sub-$29 module combos grandfathered indefinitely. Standalone
  module SKUs close to new customers at launch.
- INR: purchasing-power priced, not converted - Trade ₹999/mo with 1,000
  msgs (Bedrock cost is global; protect margin via allowance, overage ₹2/msg);
  Genie ₹3,499. Voice per-country at telco cost + 50%.
